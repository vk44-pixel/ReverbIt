Understanding Pitch-Preserved Slowing vs Tape Drop

✨ Lock Same Pitch (Preserve)

Uses Time-Stretch overlap algorithms to slow down the speed without altering the musical key. The singer does not sound like a low monster, keeping their exact natural timbre while floating over a relaxed tempo.

📼 Tape / Vinyl Slowdown

Slows the tape speed directly. In this mode, pitch drops proportionally with speed (the classic 90s cassette and chopped-and-screwed sound), creating heavy dark baritone tones.

🛁 Stereo Convolution Reverb

Synthesizes an acoustic impulse response mimicking large cathedral halls, empty bedrooms, or echoing party bathrooms with high-frequency absorption and stereo cross-feedback.
